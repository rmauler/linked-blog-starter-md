Greetings Lochmere,
The approach of fall means many changes for our wonderful Barony.  By the time you read this, our fighter practices will be moving indoors for the season and we'll only have around 4 practices left for our archery and thrown weapons practice before we have to stop for the year.  We also have new crowns, King Athos and Queen Alianora.  We weren't able to make it to coronation, but we're excited to see what they'll bring to Atlantia during their reign.  

It's also time to consider if you're interested in becoming the next Baronage of Lochmere.  Our term ends next April and we'll need letters of intent from any interested parties by mid December.  If you're interested, but not fully sure what goes into the job, feel free to reach out to us.  We're happy to walk people through what goes into into it.  

Sadly, we don't have much on the docket for October event wise.  His Excellency has finally returned after around 6 months of on and off work travel and we'll be spending some time getting our lives put back together.

I imagine most have heard by now, but for those that haven't, Baroness Graciela - Landed Baroness of Storvik, former landed Baroness of Black Diamond, Pelican, Laurel,  and Nonpareil passed away on September 20th. Her impact on Atlantia and the SCA is beyond measure. She has touched so many lives that is can hard to picture how things will be with her gone.  If you need to talk, you're always welcome to reach out to either of us. 

Hug your friends.  We're here if you need us.

Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
