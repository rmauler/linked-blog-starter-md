The Blasted Oak provides shelter for the populace with its widespread boughs, creating spaces for gathering.   Godwin has similarly spread news of our Barony far and wide through his service as our Chronicler.

His diligent note taking and design of our newsletter make it possible for all to know the goings on in our Barony.  He has also taken it upon himself to volunteer as an event steward as well training to become a marshal for thrown weapons.

Thus we, Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia, would like to make our gratitude to this service known by inducting Lord Godwin Rutherford into our order of the Blasted Oak.  Done this 25th of January A.S. LIX at our Midwinter Revel: Lunar New Year.
