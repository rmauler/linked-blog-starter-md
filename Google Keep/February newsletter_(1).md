Greetings Lochmere,
As you read this, we just finished up with another wonderful Midwinter's celebration.  Midwinter's Revel: Lunar New Year was a perfect way to start the year.  We really enjoyed the new style of feast Lady Katla and her team prepared with dishes all across East Asia.  We especially enjoyed the tea ceremony run by Lord Klaus.  Iona and Godwin did a wonderful job organizing the event and it was evident in how the decor and attention to detail enhanced the theme.  Thank to you Scott for running the A&S activities and thank you to everyone that entered the competitions or brought something to display.  

Speaking of arts, we introduced a new award for A&S to complete the series of awards we started last year.  The Award of the Eagle's Eye will be given for a singular act of great skill in the arts and or sciences.  During court, we inducted Iona and Godwin both into our Order of the Blasted Oak for all of the dedicated service they've been contributing to the Barony.  We also gave out the first 3 Eagle's Eyes to Eilionora, Katla, and one that we read into court business, but haven't given to the recipient yet.  

Lastly, we were able to honor Lady Aine for all the work she has done for Lochmere despite living outside its borders with an Azure Heron.  And as she is going to shortly leave us to move to Drachenwald, we decided to grant her honorary citizenship in Lochmere so she'll always know she's welcome in our lands.

After Midwinter's, while Her Excellency was unable to attend, I was able to go to Winter University.  I took some wonderful classes and I'm already looking forward to the next university session.  With how much Lochmeri have done for the University over the years it always make it just that much more special of an event for me.

Finally, Her Excellency and I plan on attending Aedult Swim and Ymir this month.  We hope to see you there!

-Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
