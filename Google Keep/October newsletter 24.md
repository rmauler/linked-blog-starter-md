
Greetings Lochmere,

September has been a truly trying time for our kingdom. Our hearts are heavy with so many losses and for those that are grieving in Atlantia this month. We are heartbroken by the absolute devastation Hurricane Helene has wrought upon the western reaches of our kingdom.  We can't remember a time our kingdom has suffered so greatly in such a short amount of time. If you know of anyone that needs help, please feel free to reach out and we'll do what we can.

On a lighter note Battle on the Bay was a shiny highlight of the month.  We recognized a few well deserving fighters, Walter Bowman and Kenji Yoshimoto, with the Order of the Crab's Claw - as well as Amleth for his prowess on the thrown weapons field with a Crab's Hammer.  Gabrell Fairecloughe did a fabulous job of running the event and we thanked his efforts to take on such a job with Our Award of the Sable Squirrel.  Brian de Moray also did a notable job of setting up and breaking down warranting the same recognition.  Symone de la Rochelle volunteered as our rapier MiC despite an injury and was recognized for her determination to participate despite adversity with Our Award of the Cloistered Crab.  Last, but not least, we were able to honor Duchess Kalisa with Lochmere's Baronial Award of Courtesy.  It was a beautiful event full of fighting, archery, a&s, and horses. The weather was great despite worrisome predictions earlier in the week. We are glad such a lovely event could brighten this month.

Looking forward, we will be attending crown next month . We hope October is a calm month full of lots of delightful pumpkins and less stresses.

Take care to all and if you're able, do what you can to help with some of recovery efforts from Helene's destruction.

Baroness Letia and Baron Linhart
