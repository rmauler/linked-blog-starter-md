Greetings Lochmere,

First and foremost we want to give our wishes that everyone has a safe and happy holiday season.  As the year comes to a close, so does our time as Baronage.  For those that haven't heard, letters of intent are due by December 20th for those interested in stepping up after us.  Please feel free to reach out to us if you have any questions about what exactly the job entails.  We're truly thankful for everyone that has stepped up lately to take on roles as event stewards.  It makes us so happy to see Lochmere continuing to grow and we will leave a thriving Barony for our successors.  

We're also excited with Midwinters coming up so soon.  We'll be having Her Excellency's gift exchange again this year, so keep an eye out for an announcement with details.  As it will be our penultimate court, we'd love to get recommendations for anyone you think has been deserving of an award.  

Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
