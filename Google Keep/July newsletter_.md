Greetings Lochmere, 
Pennsic is right around the corner and we're excited to see all of you there!  We'll be using a trailer instead of the truck this year and if you're available we'd love to have the help with loading it.  We've never used this trailer before, so we're unsure how much personal stuff is going to fit.  Once all of the baronial infrastructure is put on the trailer, personal tents maybe considered on a first come first serve basis only if there is any additional space.  Since that is not guaranteed, we encourage people to start making additional arrangements for all personal gear.  Announcements should be going out soon once we have a time settled on for the loading.

We'll also be announcing when our baronial Pennsic court is soon.  Please send us any potential conflicts you know of so we can make sure to accommodate as much of the Barony as possible.

-Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
