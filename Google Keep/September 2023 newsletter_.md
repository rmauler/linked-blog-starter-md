Greetings Lochmere,

It's been a busy summer!  We were really pleased with how well our demo at the Maryland Renaissance Garb and Craft Sale went.  We had a great location and a lot of interested people stop by the booth.  

Next on the calendar was Pennsic. The camp was delightful, always such an easy to spy landmark with it's big blue and white striped fence and welcoming common tent.

We had a delightful time fighting as well. We both got to fight in the rapier melee champions battle (our side won handily).  Letia did step funny in the woods battle-  and appreciated everyone's kindness as she was a little slow during the last half of war.
 
We were, as always, impressed with the thrown weapons baronial championship and proud to have our barony host such a fun event.

We also stopped by the knowne world A&S display- always a favorite.  We saw several crabs representing including Melchior, Marcus, Bacon, and various friends.

We held a court that was short and somehow managed to deflect incoming weather. The rain held off long enough for everyone to enjoy Lochmere's traditional post court tasty treats. 

We got home safe and sound.

We supported our cousins of bright hills the following weekend with a demo at 1623 Brewing that had many interested people who hopefully will connect with one of our baronies.

August was rounded out by Battle on the Bay. My goodness did all of Lochmere step up. So many of the marshal fields were run by Lochmeri as well as the arts and sciences display.  Even the kids' water battle was run by Lochmeri.  The deputy event steward had to step in when the event steward announced she was sick 2 days before the event....and since she is our chatelaine - we would say Lochmere stepped up.  We couldn't be more proud.   Special thanks to Bronwen for the gift of the tiny ship she made to help fill out our navy for the A&S competition.

Next on our calendar is our Baronial get-together at the MD renn faire this coming weekend.  We're excited to hang out with all of you and enjoy the faire in all its anachronistic glory!

Lastly, several Lochmeri had various health issues at Pennsic this year and we want to wish all of you a speedy recovery.

Good health wishes to all and we will see folk at fall SCA events.
