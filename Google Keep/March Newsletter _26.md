Greetings Lochmere, 
We have heirs!  Their Highnesses have officially announced their decision to make Master Melchior and Lady Kade the next Baron and Baroness of Lochmere.  We wish them the best of luck and wanted to again thank Dame Dealla and Mistress Arianna for for stepping up to put their names in the hat.  Even that takes a surprising amount of planning and forethought.  Following our letter in the newsletter this month will be a missive from our upcoming Baronage, so please make sure to read that as well.

We've also recently posted about some officer positions that will need to be filled soon.  Please check for the announcement on Facebook or the email list for details.  We have several officers moving out of the Barony shortly and could use the help backfilling their positions.

We have also been asked to help with the upcoming demo at awesome con.  If that sounds like something you'd be interested in, please reach out to us.

Our last Lochmere event as Baronage will be Night on the Port and we hope all of you will be able to come celebrate with us.  Windmasters Hill will even be sending a representative to defend their claim to the neighborhood of Lochmere, which clearly belongs to us.

Before that we will likely be attending both Defending the Gate and Spring Coronation.  Please visit us there and join us for some amazing events.  Look out for information regarding the upcoming a&s competition at Spring Coronation.

-Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
