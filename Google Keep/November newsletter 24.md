
Greetings Lochmere,

We have Heirs!  We just returned from a wonderful crown tourney in Spiaggia Levantina.  We saw some truly amazing fighting.  This is apparently the first time in 25 years that an unbelt(Lord Galfrid) has made it to the finals of crown tourney!  But, Count Abran was victorious and Anya and he are our new Prince and Princess.  It was also nice seeing so many Lochmeri out supporting our neighboring shire and the Kingdom.

Next weekend I'll(Linhart) be heading to Highland Forde for their Highland Havoc event.  Our very own Kara and Randvithr have volunteered to run the target archery for the event and I'm excited to see what clever targets they've come up with.

The following weekend we'll be going to Stierbach for Holiday Faire on Saturday and on Sunday, we'll be going to the Lochmere archery potluck at the on target site.

With the time change, we had our final archery practice of the season last week.  Since it was on Halloween, Kara set up pumpkins for everyone to shoot at!
