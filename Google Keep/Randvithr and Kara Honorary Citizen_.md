There comes a time when our subjects become such an integral part of our Barony that it can be easy to forget how much they contribute.  We have recently learned that Randvithr and Kara intend to leave our crabtastic Barony and it has caused us to think about the large viking sized hole they will leave when they're gone.  From running our martial activities to running our events, they have been selfless with their time and have sprouted from mere sable branches into true pillars of our community.  Thus, do we, Linhart and Letia, Baron and Baroness of Lochmere, issue them an honorary citizenship done this 6th day of September A.S. 60 at our Battle on the Bay.  Let this document stand as proof that they will always be true citizens of Lochmere and may forever call this Barony home.
