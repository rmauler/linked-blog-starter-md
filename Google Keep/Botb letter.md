
Greetings Lochmere!


Despite the heat, I had a wonderful time last week
at Battle on the Bay.  Our baronial cousins worked hard at setting up and running a wonderful event.  However, I have heard and been tagged in a post that stated not all had a wonderful time.  Multiple people have mentioned they experienced rude behavior.  The reports are all vague, but numerous enough to be disheartening to hear as a baroness in the area.  I want to remind everyone that courtesy is a priority - especially when it is hot and everyone is uncomfortable. Please consider your words and if they can be taken negatively - it can make a big difference to someone's event.

I do hope most people had a good event!
