Lochmere, as you may have heard, a number of Our Baronial officers will be moving shortly and as such we are in need of volunteers to step up for several positions.  

Chronicler: Publishing the monthly newsletter is a requirement for the continued operation of our group, so this one is probably the most pressing position we need to fill.

Knight Marshal:  This officer position can be held by any warranted Marshal for any martial discipline.  Ideally, we'd be looking for someone that comes to Tuesday practice to help with non-member waivers and loaner gear.

Minister of Arts and Sciences:  The MoAS is in charge of coordinating arts and sciences for the barony.  This could be organizing classes, meetups to work on projects or helping coordinate competitions at our events.

Target Archery Marshal:  Since archery happens at a different location from our normal practice, we like to have a dedicated officer position to help coordinate the archery and thrown weapons practice on Thursdays.  Ideally you'd be able to help organize loaner gear and help with the maintenance of our targets and other range equipment.
