Fabricator General, approximately 6 hours after making planetfall on Paradyce IV, we encountered the xenos force guarding the quantum shield generators at the Dolmen Gates.  The Necron king held a defensive position among the ruins surrounding the shield generators accompanied by multiple units of infantry and a doomsday ark.  Often our current armaments are unable to render them inoperable before they can repair non-functioning units.  We will need to resupply before reengaging.  Our sulphurhounds, dunecrawler, and skitarii vanguard established a strong position on the left flank.  The sulphurhounds were able to eliminate a squad of immortals attempting to destabilize our position on the first shield generator.  I took skitarii rangers squad beta, a unit of pteraxii skystalkers, and the kastelans along with Datasmith Reylak to try and control the center of the engagement.  The repulsor grids on the kastelans performed 63% above expectations and I would like to commend Datasmith Reylak for their optimal operation.  We suffered heavy damage in the center of the engagement and only the kastelans and Datasmith Reylak experienced minimal damage.  Tech Priest Manipulus Augrisch-Vor led skitarii rangers squad alpha and a unit of  kataphron destroyers on the left flank.  They kept the warriors and chronomancer at bay, but again, were unable to overcome their regeneration protocols.  Between the kataphrons and the heavy weapons team in squad alpha, the were able to critically damage the doomsday ark, but only towards the end of our engagement.  I would also like to commend our onager dunecrawler for destroying their King, although I don't expect the damage to last long before it's repaired as well.  I recommend the further consecration of its aiming cogitators and power focusing circuitry.  I believe it would greatly improve efficiency the next time we have to eliminate a similar threat.  The Necron durability eventually proved to great and we were forced to retreat after losing too much of our resources.  I await your next instructions.
