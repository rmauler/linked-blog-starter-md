
 
All pay heed when a great blue Heron is spotted visiting the estuaries typical of crabs and other aquatic creatures.

Even the mighty crab must pay heed to the great blue heron.  And while Sir Joscelin doesn't inhabit our marshes and water ways, his presence looms large to those that call Lochmere home.  He has long gone above and beyond in helping with our Baronial pennsic encampment as well as teaching classes for us and nuturing our fighters.  As such, do we, Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia, see fit to induct him into our Order of the Azure Heron.  Done this 31st day of January anno societatis 60 at our Midwinters Revel: The Way of The Pilgrim event.
