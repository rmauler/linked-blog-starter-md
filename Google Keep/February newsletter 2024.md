
Greetings Lochmere!

We are just returning home from The Holiday Court of Volodymyr the Great.  There is so much splendor to write about. We partook in the eating of many diverse breads and saw an array of arts on display and in the pan-slavic competition. The hall was beautifully decorated and we'd like to thank Marcus for his efforts as hall steward. The feast, orchestrated by Katla, was absolutely divine and for those who missed out, well, you truly missed out.

Our court was bittersweet as we said good bye to two long time members of Lochmere, Anne and Scholastica.  We decided to bring a tradition from other baronies and create a Honorary Citizenship award so those that have been a part of Lochmere  can be assured that can always call this barony home.

We also had the joy of recognizing some outstanding newer members of Our barony that have put in some incredible work and enthusiasm. Both Godwin and Reginald received the award of the Sable Branch as they have helped our barony grow.  We also recognized Scholastica for her excellence as a Lochmere member of many years.

Art as you know is very dear to Our hearts and we were fortunate to be able to honor two skilled artisans - Eilionora and Anne for all that they have done for the barony of Lochmere.

In addition to enjoying an amazing event this weekend we have great news. We will be staying on as baronage - with the plan to officially accept the new term at Night on the Town on April 20th!

We have had an amazing time serving as your baronage and we are glad to be joining you for another term.

We hope to see everyone there!

YIS 
Baroness Letia and Baron Linhart
