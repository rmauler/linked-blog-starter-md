We had a wonderful time this weekend at The Holiday Court of Volodymyr the Great. The hall was absolutely stunning to walk into.  Especially impressive was the huge banner painted specifically for the event by Olaus.  It was nice to have a change of pace with a laid back event.  Although with all the classes it would have been easy to fill out the day with things to do. 

We started our day meeting up with the participants of the gift exchange Her Excellency hosted where His Excellency received a small army of origami crabs that many of you saw at high table later in the day.  Her Excellency went to a delightful story telling class and then partook and was impressed with all the bread at the A&S competition.  His Excellency spent the rest of the morning getting ready for our fundraiser lunch  and would like to thank Godwin, Reginald, and Marcus for helping serve the lunch as well as Farthegn for serving as his sous chef for prepping everything ahead of time.  And thanks to everyone that bought a lunch, we were able to raise $321 to go towards our Baronial fighter practice.  After lunch His Excellency had a chance to see the A&S display and competition.  He agrees all of the breads were delicious and had a great time flipping through everyone's documentation.   

We both had a great time with the few dances we did before Her Excellency scurried off to join the silk banner class in which she absolutely learned a ton before she scurried off to judge the very impressive competition.

Court seemed to be enjoyed by all. We felt the need to create an Honorary Citizen of Lochmere award as we must say goodbye to two long time members of our Barony.  We hope Anne and Scholastica always know they have a home here. We honored both Godwin and Reginald for all of the work they are doing for our Barony by inducting them into our Order of the Sable Branch and recognize the incredible art efforts of Eilionora and Anne with their induction into the Order of the Eagles Feather. 
  
Feast was a flavorful delight created by the ever impressive Katla with full documention, earning it the prize for the A&S Pan-Slavic competition which was organized and hosted by our MoAS, Eilionora.   However, We think those who stayed for feast were the real prize winners.

Clean up was impressively quick and the whole event was clearly well organized and considered by our Event Steward, Mistress Arianna to go off without a hitch.

Vivats and a giant thank you for all those involved!
