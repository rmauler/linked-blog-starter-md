
It takes a talented team to make our Kingdom's events run smoothly.  It has come to our attention that a member of our Barony has gone above and beyond the helping of our local events to help our Kingdom.  She's been contributing through helping with the events bid committee.  Done by our hands at Pennsic 50 on August 7th anno societatis LVIII.

Baron Linhart Baroness Letia

The gift of one's time is a noble sacrifice.  When we see an individual giving it so freely we are called to act.  Lady Sarra de Vicenza has been giving, working on our Kingdom's event bids committee for some time now such that all of us can enjoy precious time with our friends and those we hold dear.  This work reflects on our Barony and shows the Kingdom the excellence of Lochmere.  Thus do we, Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia, give her our Award of Excellence.  Done this day August 7th A.S. LVIII at Pennsic 50.
