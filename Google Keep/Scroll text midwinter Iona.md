
Iona's boundless energy and enthusiasm lead her to 
become a standout member of our Barony.  Whether the task is as simple as helping our marshals with paper work or as involved as becoming our Seneschal, she is eager to do what she can to give back to the Barony.

Like the Blasted Oak, her branches create new growth through her example for others.

Therefore do we, Linhart and Letia, Baron and Baroness of Lochmere, induct Lady Iona inghean Sheamuis into our Order of the Blasted Oak.
Done this 25th of January A.S. LIX at our Midwinter Revel: Lunar New Year.
