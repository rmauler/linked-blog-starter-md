Greetings Lochmere, 

By the time you read this our Polling of Confidence should be underway.   We look forward to the opportunity to spend more time helping steward our wonderful Barony.  

We'd like to thank Reginald and Iona for stepping up as our new exchequer and deputy seneschal respectively.  And a thank you to Anne as well for her service as exchequer.

This month we're attending both Unevent and Dun Carraig's investiture.  Unevent was a large chunk of our weekend, but it's nice to help keep Atlantia running smoothly.  Please reach out to your local officers if you have any questions regarding your activities.  For anyone involved in a marshal activity, be on the lookout for updates publications from Society over the next couple months.  

We're excited to visit our neighbors to the south and welcome a new set of Cousins this coming weekend.  We'd also like to congratulate Ceridwen and Harold on a job well done and a well deserved retirement.

For those interested we're planning on holding a gift exchange again.  It will be at our midwinter's event, so we'll be posting more details about it soon.

For those of you we don't see, we hope you have a safe and happy holidays and we'll see you at the Holiday Court of Volodymyr the Great.
