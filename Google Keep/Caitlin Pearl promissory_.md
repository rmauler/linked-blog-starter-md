Wilhelm and Genevieve, by the grace of the sea, King and Queen of Atlantia, to all to whom these present Letters shall reach, Greetings.  You know that we are held by Lady Caitilín inghean Uí Ruaidhrí, our citizen of Dun Carraig, for a scroll to commemorate her inductance to our Order of the Pearl in the first year of Our reign, which we promise to pay to the aforesaid Caitilin on the Pennsic War next coming.  Witness Ourselves, in Lochmere, the eleventh day of April in the first year of Our reign.
