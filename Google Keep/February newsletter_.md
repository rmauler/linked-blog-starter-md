Greetings Lochmere,

First and foremost, we're sad for the needed cancelation of our Midwinters event, but given the state of the weather, we think it was the right call.  The ice around the site has been treacherous in previous years, so we can only imagine how bad it would have been this year.  We know how much work has been going into preparing the event this year and fortunately we should be able to carry most of it forward to next year.

In case anyone missed it, the polling to determine our successors is officially underway.  It will be running until February 14th and if you have any issues, feel free to reach out.  Letia and I can try to help, but likely your concerns will need to go through either the regional seneschal or if it's ESP related, Master Jonathas.

This coming month you can expect to see us at Bright Hills Baronial Birthday, Aedult Swim, and Ymir.  We hope to see you all there too!

-Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
