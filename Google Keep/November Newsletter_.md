Greetings Lochmere,

Fall has come to the Barony!  By the time you read this out fighter practice and a&s meetup will have moved inside for the season and we'll have just had our final archery and thrown weapons practice for the year.  

We had a wonderful time at Coronation this past month.  It was a great capstone moment for Afshin and Jasmine's first reign. It was also a pleasure to watch their Majesties' Ragnarr and Mary Isobel  recieve the crowns of Atlantia. 

We'd also like to congratulate Master Fernfael for winning the day's fencing tourney and Amleth for winning the first Atlantian thrown weapons championship.  Amleth has also been named Her Majesties' thrown weapons champion! 

 We're looking forward to their Majesties' crown tournament this coming weekend and are excited to see you all there.  Good luck to all Lochmeri that are competing.  

Lochmere is staying busy as the days are getting shorter. Ferndale practice was a great success. Holiday faire is coming up and typically one of our favorite non-lochmere event. And of course there are preparations ongoing for the midwinter's event.

We hope to see everyone soon at practice or an event. Have a great fall.

-Baron Linhart and Baroness Letia
