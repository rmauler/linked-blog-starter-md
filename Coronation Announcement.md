Greetings fencers of Atlantia.  I'll be running fencing activities at this upcoming Coronation.  I have two tournaments planned for the day's activities.  First, immediately following morning court, we'll be running a tournament to select the new First Blade of Atlantia.  This tournament will be single elimination, single sword, and with all doubles refought.  Next, we'll be having a hunt where fencers will face an assortment of beasts straight from the pages of a manuscript.  The fencer to best the greatest number of them will be declared the victor.  If multiple fencers best the entire gallery of beasts, they will all be declared victorious.  We will also have a titan prize to be awarded to the fencer who places the highest.  For this, we'll be using the number of attempts to complete the challenge as a tiebreaker.
